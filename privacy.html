Privacy Policy

Mud Engineer Pro · Last updated: 6 October 2026

Mud Engineer Pro ("the app") is a drilling-fluids field calculator, training and reference tool published by IM Northstar Labs. We take your privacy seriously. In short: the app works fully offline and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, name, email, phone number, or location. It does not contain analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

To make the app useful, some information is saved only on your own device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Your favourite and recently-used calculators, and your light/dark theme choice.
  • Data you type into the report generators (Daily Mud Report, Mud Proposal, Hydraulics Report) and any company logo you add.
  • Your Pro-unlock status.

This data stays in the app's local storage. Removing the app deletes it. We cannot see or access it.

Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is processed by the app store you installed from — Google Play (Google Play Billing) or the Apple App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your card or payment details. Those transactions are governed by the store's own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Permissions

The app requests only what the purchase flow needs (the billing permission). It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files beyond letting you pick an image for a report logo, which stays on your device.

Children

The app is a professional tool intended for a general/adult audience. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
