Frequently asked

Does the app need the internet?

No. Everything — calculators, training, the manual and the report generators — works fully offline. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

The app is free with a set of core calculators and the full reference manual. A one-time Pro unlock opens all 50+ calculators, all training courses, the DMR / Mud Proposal / Hydraulics report generators, and PDF & slide export. It's a one-time purchase — no subscription.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here?

Open the Unlock Pro screen and tap Restore purchase. As long as you're signed in to the same App Store / Google account, Pro will re-activate at no charge.

Are the calculations reliable?

Each calculator shows the formula and the worked steps so you can check the method. Mud Engineer Pro is an engineering aid — always follow your company's mud programme and the operator's instructions in the field.

How do I request a feature or report a problem?

Email himageo2@gmail.com with your device model and app version (shown on the home screen).
